# D5: the first real corpus — Vietnamese public documents

Measured 2026-10-04 with `scripts/bench_k2.py WORK --sets vi_public` (raw numbers: `d5.json`;
the 30 live answers with their sources and spans: `d5_answers.jsonl`), operonx main 1.14.0 with
evals E1–E6, the same models and machine as `k2.md` (embedder `intfloat/multilingual-e5-small`,
reranker `cross-encoder/mmarco-mMiniLMv2-L12-H384-v1`, torch on 8 CPU threads, other services
running). Intervals are operonx's (`stats.estimate`: Wilson for 0/1); comparisons are operonx's
paired tests (`stats.compare_paired`: McNemar/Newcombe for 0/1, paired bootstrap otherwise).

## The corpus (`scripts/prepare_vi_public.py`)

| source | license (checked) | documents | cases |
|---|---|---|---|
| MLQA vi, `dev` (context-vi, question-vi): Vietnamese Wikipedia paragraphs, human-written questions (crowdsourced, professionally translated), human-annotated answer spans | CC BY-SA 3.0 (MLQA README), `MLQA_V1.zip` SHA-256 `246e8089…` | 228 HTML | 511 |
| MLQA vi, `test` articles not in `dev` (distractors) | same | 400 HTML | — |
| Official legal PDFs, vanban.chinhphu.vn (`robots.txt`: `Allow: /`) | not protected by copyright (Law on IP 2005, Art. 15); listed with SHA-256 in `datasets/vi_public_legal.jsonl` | 63 PDF (970 pages) | — |

691 documents, 3727 chunks; ingest took 17.5 min on CPU (PDF parsing and embedding).

- Each case's label is the sentence of the paragraph holding the dataset's own answer start (the
  human-annotated span widened to its sentence); the expected answer is the answer text. No label
  was written by hand. All 511 labels resolved.
- The legal PDFs have no cases: no human-written Vietnamese legal QA set with a verifiable license
  was found. UIT-ViQuAD 2.0, BKAI legal retrieval and Zalo AI 2021 legal retrieval state no license
  or only an uploader's tag; their upstream terms could not be verified, so they are not used.
  They are real PDFs in the same collection: the eval measures retrieval among them.
- Of the first 200 legal PDFs crawled, 137 (68%) were scans without a text layer. They are left
  out: there is no OCR yet. That is the largest gap a real Vietnamese government corpus shows.
- No news sites (copyrighted, terms against reuse).

## Gate on the real corpus: hybrid stays the default

| mode | R@5 | R@10 (95% CI) | R@20 | MRR | nDCG@10 | p50 ms | p95 ms |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| dense | 0.744 | **0.793** [0.755, 0.826] | 0.836 | 0.641 | 0.676 | 110 | 124 |
| lexical | 0.779 | **0.836** [0.801, 0.865] | 0.865 | 0.670 | 0.709 | 88 | 113 |
| hybrid | 0.830 | **0.861** [0.828, 0.888] | 0.910 | 0.730 | 0.759 | 176 | 206 |
| hybrid+rerank | 0.855 | **0.881** [0.850, 0.906] | 0.914 | 0.785 | 0.806 | 4942 | 6062 |

Unlike the K2 sets, this one is not saturated, and it separates the modes:

- **Hybrid vs dense**, Recall@10 +0.069 [+0.042, +0.096], McNemar p < 0.001 (43 cases fixed,
  8 lost); MRR +0.088 [+0.065, +0.112]. Hybrid stays the default.
- **Hybrid vs lexical**, Recall@10 +0.025 [−0.002, +0.053], p = 0.085: lexical alone is a strong
  baseline on Vietnamese Wikipedia, but hybrid is not worse anywhere.
- **Rerank pays here**, unlike on the K2 sets: MRR +0.055 [+0.029, +0.081] (bootstrap, p = 0.001),
  Recall@10 +0.020 [−0.004, +0.044] (p = 0.13). At ~5 s per query on CPU it stays opt-in
  (`reranker=`); with a GPU, or a smaller candidate depth, it is worth turning on.

## Analyzer: `vi` + folding, with a cost on accented questions

| analyzer | R@10 accented | MRR accented | R@10 unaccented | MRR unaccented |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| simple | 0.834 | 0.707 | 0.487 | 0.345 |
| simple+fold | 0.775 | 0.632 | 0.775 | 0.632 |
| vi | 0.836 | 0.672 | 0.489 | 0.342 |
| vi+fold | 0.836 | 0.670 | 0.836 | 0.670 |

`vi`+fold keeps accented Recall@10 and takes unaccented questions from 0.49 to 0.84, as on the K2
sets. Real text shows a cost the synthetic sets hid: accented MRR is 0.04 lower than `simple`
(bigrams add noise to the ranking of well-typed questions). `vi`+fold stays the recommendation
for a Vietnamese collection, because an unaccented question loses 42% of its answers without
folding; indexing both forms is the next thing to measure if accented MRR matters.

Postgres FTS (`ts_rank_cd`) on this corpus: Recall@10 0.301, MRR 0.163, against FTS5 `bm25`'s
0.836 / 0.670. Without IDF, the long legal PDFs full of common words win every OR query.
Postgres FTS should not be a default ranker (`k2.md`).

The one drop is an English name given for a Vietnamese source ("the Lee Strasberg Theatre

sentence of the right paragraph rather than the gold one, or retrieval missed the paragraph
(hybrid Recall@5 is 0.83). It measures agreement with one gold sentence, not correctness.

## The quote tolerance (PLAN R6)

Proposed and implemented (`retrieval/citations.py`, `TOLERANCES`): a quote still verifies when it
differs from the source only in **the kind of quotation mark** or **the case of its first letter**.
The citation records which tolerance it needed and always shows the canonical text at its span,
never the model's string.

Evidence:

- K2 live check: 2 of the 5 dropped citations were exactly these differences (checked by hand
  against the canonical text): `"design build"` written as `'design build'` (the model escaping
  its JSON), and a sentence quoted with its first letter lowercased. Both quoted the right text.
- D5 live check: 0 of 30 citations needed a tolerance, and the one drop stays dropped.
- Everything else is still refused: a removed quote mark, a case change inside the quote, other
  words, a translation (unit tests in `tests/unit/test_citations.py`).

The tolerance can only turn a citation into one that points at the exact source text it
paraphrased by punctuation or capitalisation, so it cannot show text that is not there.
`verify_citations(tolerances=())` is the strict verifier.
