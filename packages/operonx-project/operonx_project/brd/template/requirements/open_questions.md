# Open questions, ambiguities, conflicts and divergences — `Q-nn`

Each entry separates **what the sources say** (fact) from **our interpretation** (proposal) and records who can
decide. A resolved entry stays here with its resolution; it is never deleted.

Types: **ambiguity** (a source is unclear) · **conflict** (two statements disagree) · **divergence** (the product
differs from a source, knowingly) · **gap** (required, not built).
